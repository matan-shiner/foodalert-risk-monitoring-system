No workaround was attempted (no alternate mirrors, caches, or third-party
fetchers were used), consistent with this environment's own proxy guidance
that a 403 means the destination is not allowed for this session and should
be reported rather than routed around.

2026-09-12 run hit the identical `connect_rejected` / 403 policy denial on
both hosts. Two weeks in a row of the same specific denial indicates this is
not a transient blip but a standing egress-policy gap that needs an
administrator to add `efoodalert.com` and `www.foodsafetynews.com` to the
allowlist.
